version=0.17.2

about=Detects forest cover changes between two dates (or a whole time series): the change mask is computed by a neural model - a compact CNN over the multi-date volume with a cross-attention 3x3 neighbourhood (centre queries its 9 neighbours, 8 heads, validity mask) and a bidirectional CfC recurrent branch (length-aware pooling, dynamic T 2-8 dates), temporal feature summaries (organizers F1 0.929, P 0.951 / recall 0.907) - and a TypeNet (distilled DOFA-style 12x768 encoder + cross-attention readout over the dates, val macro-F1 0.911 on changed chips; vozh logging F1 0.949, windfall 0.693) classifies the type of the changed 32 px (320 m) chips (logging/windfall/fire). Urban/water/bare pixels are excluded via Sentinel-2 SCL and an NDVI vegetation check. Outputs: class raster, merged change polygons and a cells layer (one polygon per changed cell with coordinates, type and confidence). Runs on CPU (onnxruntime).
